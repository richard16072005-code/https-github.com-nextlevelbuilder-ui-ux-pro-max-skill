    Aftercare & FAQs

    Keep them looking fresh

    How long will my nails last?

    Gel polish lasts 2 to 3 weeks. BIAB, acrylic and gel extensions are best infilled every 3 weeks so the nail stays strong and balanced.

    Which tier is my inspo picture?

    Plain colour, French and glitter are Tier 1. Chrome, ombré and simple line art are Tier 2. Hand-painted pictures, 3D charms, heavy gems or three or more different designs in one set are Tier 3. Send your photo and Ev will confirm before you book.

    Can I come in with another tech's set on?

    Yes. Add “Removal” so there's time to soak off safely without damaging your natural nails.

    What should I do after my appointment?

    Use cuticle oil every day, wear gloves for cleaning and washing up, and don't use your nails as tools. If anything lifts in the first 5 days, message Ev for a free fix.

    Do you take deposits?

    A small deposit holds your slot and comes off your final price. Please give 24 hours' notice if you need to move your appointment.
