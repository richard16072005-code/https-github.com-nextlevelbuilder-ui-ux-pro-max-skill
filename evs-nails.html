Aftercare & FAQs

Keep them looking fresh

How long will my nails last?

Gel polish lasts 2 to 3 weeks. BIAB, acrylic and gel extensions are best infilled every 3 weeks so the balance of the nail stays strong.

Which tier is my inspo picture?

As a guide: plain colour, French and a single accent nail are Tier 1. Chrome, ombré and simple line art are Tier 2. Hand-painted pictures, 3D charms, heavy gems or every nail different is Tier 3. Send your photo and Ev will confirm before you book.

Can I come in with another tech's set on?

Yes. Add "Removal of another tech's work" so there's enough time to soak off safely without damaging your natural nails.

What should I do after my appointment?

Apply cuticle oil every day, wear gloves for cleaning and washing up, and don't use your nails as tools. If anything lifts in the first 5 days, message Ev for a free fix.

Do you take deposits?

A small deposit holds your slot and comes off your final price. Please give 24 hours' notice if you need to move your appointment.
