Two ways to give flowers

Fresh for the day, or forever for the shelf

Every design in the shop comes both ways. Pick fresh for scent and the real thing, or forever for a keepsake that never wilts.

Fresh blooms

Real seasonal flowers, picked and arranged close to your date.

  • Scent, texture and that just-cut look
  • Lasts about 7–10 days in a vase
  • Best for the day itself: ceremonies, dates, surprises

Comes with a care card: trim stems, fresh water every two days.

Forever faux

Premium silk and foam flowers, styled to look real and made to last.

  • No water, no wilting, no pollen
  • Keeps for years on a desk or shelf
  • Can be made weeks ahead, perfect for busy dates

Dust gently with a hairdryer on the cool setting to keep it bright.

Questions

Good to know

How far ahead should I order?

For fresh bouquets, a few days' notice is ideal. For Valentine's Day, Mother's Day and graduation season, order as early as you can since spots fill up. Forever bouquets can be made weeks in advance.

Can you match school or wedding colours?

Yes. Send a photo or the colour names with your order and Belle will match them as closely as possible. Faux flowers come in more shades, so they're great for exact colour matches.

What's the difference in price between fresh and forever?

Forever bouquets cost a little more because the faux stems are reusable keepsakes. Both prices show on every design, and the builder updates the price as you go.

Do you deliver?

Pick up is always available, and local delivery can be arranged. Mention your suburb in the order form and Belle will confirm.

Can I add something that isn't listed?

Of course. Use the notes box in the order form for anything special: a photo print, chocolates, a plushie, a specific flower.
