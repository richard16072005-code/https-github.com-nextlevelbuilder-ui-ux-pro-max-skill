Our Process

Simple. Transparent. Managed.

One point of contact from the first conversation to the final walk-through.

01

Consultation

We meet on site, listen to your goals, assess the space and talk budget and timing.

02

Design & Quote

You get a clear, itemised quote with design input, materials and a realistic schedule.

03

Build

Our trades deliver the work on a managed program, with regular updates and a clean site.

04

Handover

A detailed walk-through, final touches, and a space ready to use from day one.
