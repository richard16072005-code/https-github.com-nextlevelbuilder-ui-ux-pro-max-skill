FAQ

Common Questions

What does "rental arbitrage" actually mean?

We lease your property at a fixed monthly rate, just like a traditional tenant, then operate it as a short-term rental on Airbnb and similar platforms. The difference between what we pay you and what we earn from guests is our margin — but you're guaranteed your rent either way.

Does it cost me anything to list my property?

No. We cover furnishing, photography, listing creation, cleaning, and day-to-day management. There is zero upfront cost to you as the owner.

What if my HOA or city doesn't allow short-term rentals?

We run a full compliance check before signing any lease, including HOA bylaws and local short-term rental regulations, so you're never exposed to legal risk.

How do real estate agents get compensated?

Agents who refer a qualifying property that results in a signed lease receive a one-time referral commission, paid at signing — on top of any commission already due from the landlord.

What happens if a guest damages the property?

Every stay is backed by platform host guarantees plus our own $1M liability insurance policy. Owners are never responsible for guest-caused damage.
